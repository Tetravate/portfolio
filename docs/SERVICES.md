## Overview

Tetravate organizes its studio capabilities into **six official service categories**. These six categories serve as the only top-level service categories across the studio's website, services page, contact forms, and project case study mappings.

Each category retains our existing technical capabilities and sub-services, nested under their appropriate parent category without loss of technical detail.

---

## The Six Official Service Categories

### 1. Websites & Digital Experiences
* **Identifier:** `websites-digital-experiences`
* **Short Description:** Modern websites, landing pages, digital storefront introductions, and user-centric interfaces engineered for responsive performance.
* **Overview:** We design and develop bespoke digital experiences that communicate what a business does clearly and credibly. Every project is built from the ground up with semantic markup, responsive layouts, search engine meta configuration, and clean visual structure.
* **Nested Capabilities & Sub-Services:**
  * **Websites and landing pages:** Fast, focused web pages engineered for clarity, product introductions, and campaign messaging.
  * **Business and portfolio websites:** Structured multi-page company sites that articulate brand identity, showcase work, and guide prospective clients toward clear contact actions.
  * **UI/UX design and redesigns:** Clean, mobile-first and desktop-refined layouts built with legible typography, strong contrast, purposeful micro-interactions, state transitions, and usability redesigns that eliminate navigation friction.
* **Appropriate For:** Companies, studios, and founders launching a new venture or upgrading an outdated web presence with high visual and structural clarity.
* **Verified Studio Demonstration:** MĀRA Dining (`mara-dining`) & Oivu Prototype (`oivu`).

---

### 2. E-Commerce Solutions
* **Identifier:** `ecommerce-solutions`
* **Short Description:** Online stores, structured product catalogues, and checkout workflows engineered for dependable retail transactions.
* **Overview:** We engineer e-commerce solutions that make product browsing intuitive and purchasing seamless. We focus on catalog clarity, responsive cart management, customer account portals, and robust order workflows.
* **Nested Capabilities & Sub-Services:**
  * **Online stores:** Full-featured digital storefronts tailored to merchandise, brand identity, and multi-device shopping.
  * **Product catalogues:** Structured category navigation, faceted product filtering, and detailed product showcase displays.
  * **Shopping carts:** Persistent cart calculations, state synchronization, and frictionless bag interactions.
  * **Checkout integrations:** Streamlined customer address handling, order review flows, payment gateway connections, and clear checkout completion steps.
  * **Order management systems:** Order placement records, customer purchase histories, shipment status tracking, and administrative store oversight.
* **Appropriate For:** Retail brands, makers, and merchants requiring a modern digital storefront with clear order lifecycle visibility.
* **Verified Studio Demonstration:** LUXORA — Premium E-Commerce (`tetravate-ecommerce`).

---

### 3. Web Applications & Systems
* **Identifier:** `web-applications-systems`
* **Short Description:** Custom browser applications, operational portals, and management software tailored to complex business requirements.
* **Overview:** When off-the-shelf software doesn't fit existing operational workflows, we build bespoke web applications. We prioritize deterministic business logic, responsive performance, role-based access control, and maintainable codebase architectures.
* **Nested Capabilities & Sub-Services:**
  * **Custom web applications:** Bespoke web applications built around specific business logic, data structures, and operational team requirements.
  * **Admin panels:** Role-governed administrative backoffices for data oversight, user permissions, audit tracking, and team management.
  * **Management systems:** Centralized systems for organizing inventory, enterprise resources, institutional roles, and internal operations.
  * **Booking and reservation platforms:** Interactive scheduling tools, availability calendars, and customer appointment management systems.
* **Appropriate For:** Operational teams, startups, and institutions seeking customized digital tools to manage internal records or customer self-service workflows.
* **Verified Studio Demonstration:** Nivara ERP (`nivara-erp`) & AGS Masalas (`ags-masalas`).

---

### 4. AI & Data Solutions
* **Identifier:** `ai-data-solutions`
* **Short Description:** Practical AI-powered applications, intelligent assistants, machine learning, and data analytics dashboards designed for operational utility.
* **Overview:** We explain artificial intelligence and data science in accessible language, focusing on practical tasks where intelligent automation and data clarity genuinely help. Through clear charts, geospatial mapping, and centralized dashboards, we turn disparate record sets into transparent visual summaries.
* **Nested Capabilities & Sub-Services:**
  * **AI-powered applications:** Software integrating practical machine intelligence, structured prompt handling, and automated text or workflow assistance.
  * **Chatbots and intelligent assistants:** Context-aware conversational interfaces for customer inquiry support, internal knowledge assistance, or prompt execution.
  * **Machine learning:** Structured pattern analysis, anomaly detection, and classification based on historical domain data and deterministic engineering rules.
  * **Data analysis:** Transforming numerical records and relational datasets into legible graphical representations and summary charts.
  * **Business insights:** Operational metric dashboards providing real-time visibility into sales, activity logs, geospatial clusters, and key performance indicators.
* **Appropriate For:** Organizations looking to automate repetitive classification/inquiry tasks or visualize complex operational records and datasets.
* **Verified Studio Demonstration:** SAKSHA / DRISHYAM (`saksha`), MISTIQ 2.0 (`mistiq`), & AGA AI Assistant (`aga-ai-assistant`).

---

### 5. Backend & Integrations
* **Identifier:** `backend-integrations`
* **Short Description:** Solid database schemas, structured APIs, authentication logic, server architecture, and third-party integrations.
* **Overview:** Backend systems support the functionality, data handling, access control, and integrations required by digital products. We architect clear database schemas, build maintainable APIs, and implement secure authentication, focusing on data integrity and dependable external service connectivity.
* **Nested Capabilities & Sub-Services:**
  * **API development:** Clean, documented RESTful services and endpoint architectures connecting frontends to backend logic.
  * **Database design:** Relational and document data modeling, schema normalization, indexing for query performance, and reliable state preservation.
  * **Authentication:** Secure user registration, session management, OAuth, role-based access control (RBAC), and permission boundaries.
  * **Application logic:** Server-side business rules, background processing, validation, and transaction pipelines.
  * **Third-party integrations:** Payment processors, messaging services (WhatsApp, email), cloud storage providers, and external software APIs.
* **Appropriate For:** Products requiring multi-user access, complex data relationships, dependable authentication, and custom business logic.
* **Verified Studio Demonstration:** Nivara ERP (`nivara-erp`) & Tetravate Workspace (`tetravate-workspace`).

---

### 6. Deployment & Technical Support
* **Identifier:** `deployment-technical-support`
* **Short Description:** Production releases, code testing, performance tuning, bug fixes, and ongoing technical support for digital reliability.
* **Overview:** Developing a product also involves making it available for use, verifying its behavior across devices, correcting issues, and improving performance. We assist with production hosting, DNS configuration, testing, performance optimization, and dependable post-launch support.
* **Nested Capabilities & Sub-Services:**
  * **Website deployment:** Setting up production hosting environments, DNS records, SSL certificates, and automated deployment pipelines.
  * **Application testing:** Cross-device verification, end-to-end workflow testing, regression checks, and accessibility compliance.
  * **Bug fixes:** Investigating runtime errors, edge cases, data inconsistencies, and resolving software defects.
  * **Performance improvements:** Auditing bundle sizes, optimizing asset delivery, caching strategies, reducing load times, and ensuring smooth browser responsiveness.
  * **Ongoing technical support:** Direct founder communication, post-launch maintenance, troubleshooting, and evolutionary software enhancements.
* **Appropriate For:** Clients preparing to launch a product or seeking to test, stabilize, and maintain existing web assets.
* **Verified Studio Demonstration:** Live Cloud Deployments & AGS Masalas In-Store Field Rollout (`ags-masalas`).
